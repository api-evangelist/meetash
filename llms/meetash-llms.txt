# MeetAsh (Ash / Ash Wellness)

> Ash is a New York-based health technology company, founded in 2019, that powers
> white-label at-home health testing programs for health plans, digital health
> companies, and public health organizations. A single REST API integration lets a
> partner trigger test kits, manage shipping logistics, and transmit results back
> into an EHR or patient portal across a national network of CLIA- and CAP-certified
> laboratories. Ash offers 120+ at-home diagnostic panels (hormone, cancer, allergy,
> STI, chronic condition and infectious disease, from self-collected blood, stool,
> urine, saliva and swab samples), care-gap-closure programs for HEDIS measures,
> kitting and fulfillment, physician-of-record services, and reporting dashboards.
> The platform is HIPAA compliant and SOC 2 Type II certified.

## Status of the developer surface

Ash markets a REST API but does not publish it. The developer documentation at
https://docs.ashwellness.io is a ReadMe hub in password-protected mode: every path
302s to /password, and its robots.txt is "User-agent: * / Disallow: /". No OpenAPI,
AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Postman collection, MCP server, or agent card is published at
any public Ash URL, and no first-party SDK exists in npm, PyPI, or a public GitHub
organization. Access to the contract requires a partner conversation via
https://www.poweredbyash.com/contact-us.
